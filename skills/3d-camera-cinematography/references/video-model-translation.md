# Translating camera design into AI video instructions

Use this reference when a shot is generated by an image-to-video or text-to-video model. The
model provider, endpoint, API key, account and execution environment are configured outside this
Skill by the user.

## 1. Decide whether AI video is appropriate

Good candidates:
- lifestyle use scenes
- human interaction and emotional reaction
- atmospheric establishing shots
- environment movement and transitions
- non-critical product beauty shots where exact geometry is not the main claim

Prefer Blender or verified source footage for:
- exact internal product construction
- component counts and placement
- mechanical assembly/disassembly
- precise fluid/air/heat/electric paths
- exact dimensions, timing, or causal demonstrations

If the generated shot makes a scientific claim, inspect the result frame by frame or use another
method that can guarantee the required geometry.

## 2. Prompt construction order

Write prompts in this order:
1. **Language and performance lock**: Simplified Chinese speech, voiceover, dialogue and visible text; tone and delivery.
2. **Subject lock**: identity, product silhouette, color, materials, proportions, distinctive parts.
3. **Action**: one primary action, with clear start → middle → end.
4. **Environment**: location, time, background and relevant props.
5. **Shot size and angle**: exact framing and viewpoint.
6. **Camera movement**: camera move, direction, path and speed profile.
7. **Focus and composition**: focus target, depth of field, subject position, safe area.
8. **Lighting**: key/fill/rim, direction, softness, reflections and exposure.
9. **Style**: realistic cinematic 3D science documentary; retain the approved visual bible.
10. **Continuity**: match supplied reference image and adjacent shot state.
11. **Negative constraints**: no geometry drift, morphing, extra parts, text artifacts, camera
    shake, unexpected cuts, object teleportation or contradictory physical behavior.

## 3. Language and voice direction (mandatory)

Unless the user explicitly requests another language, the finished video must use **Simplified
Chinese throughout**:
- All narration, voiceover, spoken dialogue and character speech must be natural Mandarin Chinese.
- All subtitles, titles, labels, captions, diagrams and other audience-readable text must be
  correct Simplified Chinese.
- Do not generate English narration, English dialogue, bilingual speech, random Latin letters,
  pseudo-text, garbled Chinese, or invented brand marks.
- Preserve verified brand names, model numbers and standard abbreviations only when genuinely
  needed; explain unfamiliar technical terms in Chinese.
- Internal filenames, Blender object names, collection names and production metadata may remain
  English. They are not audience-facing content.

Voice and writing tone:
- Curious, lively, friendly and lightly humorous, like a knowledgeable science communicator
  telling an interesting story.
- Use conversational Mandarin, natural pauses, varied sentence rhythm and clear emphasis.
- Use a hook, a rhetorical question, a relatable analogy or a small witty turn when it helps
  understanding.
- Keep humor subtle and purposeful. Avoid forced jokes, meme overload, childish baby talk,
  exaggerated claims, sales-pitch delivery or dry textbook/technical-manual narration.
- Scientific facts, numbers, causality and safety guidance must remain precise.
- Default narration remains the main storytelling track. Character dialogue may add natural
  interaction but must not compete with or replace the narration unless requested.

Suggested voice direction for a TTS or voice model:
"普通话，中文母语者自然表达；语气亲切、轻松、好奇，带一点机智和幽默感；像一位会讲故事的科普博主，而不是播报员。语速中等偏快，重点词有自然重音，句间留有呼吸和思考停顿；吐字清楚，不夸张、不油腻、不装可爱。"

## 4. Make camera motion unambiguous

Weak: "cinematic slow camera movement around a hair dryer".

Better:
"One continuous 8-second three-quarter product shot. The hair dryer remains stationary and keeps
the same silhouette, proportions and surface finish. The camera starts at a front-left three-quarter
medium close-up, makes a restrained 20-degree clockwise arc while moving 10 cm closer, and ends on
a stable close-up of the air outlet. Constant subject orientation; smooth ease-in and ease-out;
no orbit beyond the stated arc; no cuts."

Use numbers only when they are useful and plausible. If the model cannot reliably honor exact
distances/angles, describe the intended screen-space change and inspect the output rather than
claiming numerical precision.

## 5. Start and end frame continuity

For each shot specify:
- reference image or visual bible identifier
- opening composition
- final composition
- subject orientation and action phase
- camera direction
- what must remain unchanged
- what may change

When adjacent clips must join, plan a matching final/initial pose, movement direction, framing,
lighting and background. Avoid relying on an unconstrained model to remember a previous clip.

## 6. Copy-ready prompt template

Adapt every timeline to the actual duration. Each generated shot must be a single continuous shot
longer than zero and no longer than 15 seconds.

```text
[镜头编号 / 时长]
单个连续镜头，时长：X 秒（必须 ≤15 秒）。不要擅自增加切镜或转场。

[语言与声音——强制]
最终成片所有语音均使用自然普通话中文：第三人称中文旁白为主；如有角色对白，也必须为中文。
旁白语气轻松、好奇、有故事感，带一点恰到好处的幽默，像会讲故事的科普博主；
不要说明书腔、新闻播报腔、低幼卖萌或刻意搞笑。
所有字幕、标题、标签和画面内可读文字均使用正确简体中文。
禁止英文语音、英文字幕、随机字母、伪文字、乱码和模型臆造 Logo。
如果模型不能稳定生成汉字，画面中不要生成任何文字，留出干净区域供后期添加中文字幕。

[主体锁定]
主体身份、外观轮廓、颜色、材质、比例、关键部件保持一致：……

[动作时间线]
0–Xs：……
……（按镜头总时长调整）

[场景与空间布局]
地点、主体位置、前景/中景/背景、道具位置、空间关系：……

[摄影机]
景别：
机位/视角：
焦距/透视：
起始构图：
运动路径、方向与速度：
结束构图：
焦点：

[构图与光线]
主体画面占比、字幕安全区、主光/补光/轮廓光、材质反射：……

[视觉风格]
写实电影级 3D 科普纪录片质感，产品结构可信，电影级布光，真实材质和自然景深。

[连续性]
与参考图及前后镜头保持一致：主体外观、朝向、位置、动作状态、光线和环境……

[负面约束]
无切镜、无变形、无多余部件、无结构漂移、无错误物理过程、无镜头抖动、
无物体瞬移、无随机文字、无英文字幕、无错误汉字、无虚构 Logo。
```

For systems that separate video and audio generation, keep the same Chinese voice direction in
the audio/TTS prompt and generate or proofread subtitles separately. Do not assume that a visual
video prompt alone guarantees spoken Chinese.

## 7. Text rendering strategy

Video models frequently produce unstable Chinese typography. Use this decision order:
1. If the model has verified, reliable text rendering, generate only short Chinese labels and
   inspect every character.
2. Otherwise, generate clean footage with no visible text and reserve safe areas.
3. Add titles, subtitles, labels and diagrams in the editing/compositing stage using a Chinese
   font with correct glyph coverage.
4. Proofread text against the approved script. Never accept pseudo-Chinese or approximate glyphs.

## 8. Generation and review loop

1. Produce one shot or a small coherent batch; do not generate the whole episode blindly.
2. Review opening, middle, ending and any frame containing a scientific fact.
3. Listen to the audio: verify Mandarin pronunciation, wording, tone, pacing and absence of
   unexpected English speech.
4. Inspect every visible text element for correct Simplified Chinese; remove or replace malformed
   text in post.
5. Reject identity drift, part-count changes, geometry morphing, camera path violations, unwanted
   cuts, unreadable action and incorrect physical behavior.
6. Compare with the reference image and adjacent approved shots.
7. Revise one major prompt variable at a time.
8. If repeated generations cannot meet geometric accuracy, switch that shot to Blender or a
   hybrid workflow.

A fluent prompt is not evidence that the model obeyed it. The generated media is the source of truth
for acceptance.
